# Parampara Path

## Register
product

## Platform
web

## Purpose
A last-mile learner loop for Indian living heritage: place or theme → source-attributed card → grounded Q&A that can refuse → short quiz → contribution that stays unpublished until a human accepts it.

## Users
Primary: 18–24 Indian students on laptop or mid-range Android Chrome.
Secondary: curious travellers using the same responsive web app.

## Positioning
Unofficial learner tool. Not the national archive. Do not compete with the Ministry of Culture / Indian Culture Portal. Point to official pages; own the eight-minute journey.

## Jury surface
`/about` analyses users (personas) and UR-01…UR-12 with acceptance tests. `/me` (Analysis) shows live Met / Partial / Ready / Not yet from Ask, Quiz, and contribution signals. Primary screens show which UR they satisfy plus the five-step loop stepper.

## Brand personality
Reliable reference tool. Calm, neutral, cited. The heritage lives in the content and its sources, not in decorative cultural styling. Trust is shown, not claimed: source counts, check dates, grounded vs not-covered answers, human review. AI is a retrieval interface, not an author of culture.

## Anti-references
AR/VR demos, culture Reels, student encyclopedia clones, ungrounded chatbots, cream/sand “heritage” templates, exoticising ornament (textile patterns, mandalas, lotus/diya/rangoli motifs, decorative Devanagari, spice-named palettes).

## Accessibility
WCAG 2.2 AA intent, no autoplay of sacred sound, omit restricted practices, English UI for this local MVP.
